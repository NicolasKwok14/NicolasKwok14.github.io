I designed and built an electronic Heads or Tails game from the ground up, integrating a custom-soldered circuit with red and green LEDs to randomly indicate an outcome. The electronics are housed in a vacuum-formed polystyrene enclosure with a laser-cut acrylic interface, combining circuit assembly, soldering, vacuum forming, and laser cutting into a compact functional product.
