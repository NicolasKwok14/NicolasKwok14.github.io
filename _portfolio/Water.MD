I designed and built an automatic plant watering system to solve a real problem for a client who travels frequently and struggles to keep her indoor herb plants alive while away. The final product features an adjustable timer circuit, a refillable 2L water tank, and a compact injection-molded ABS housing, evolving from an early Arduino-based prototype into a commercially viable design ready for batch production. The project combined client research, circuit design, 3D printing, and manufacturing analysis into a single functional product.

# Features
* **Automatic, Adjustable Watering.** A 12V relay timer circuit controls a mini water pump on a set schedule, letting the watering frequency and duration be adjusted to match the plant's needs and prevent over- or under-watering.
* **Client-Driven Design Process.** Developed through direct client research and interviews, iterating through 6 initial concepts and 3 refined prototypes before arriving at a final design tailored to a real user's space, plants, and travel habits.
* **Refillable 2L Water Tank.** A food-grade polypropylene tank with a screwable lid holds enough water to keep plants hydrated for up to 3 weeks without refilling, addressing the client's frequent travel schedule.
* **Compact, Waterproof Housing.** A 3D-printed PLA enclosure with a laser-cut acrylic user interface houses the circuit and LED indicators, keeping all electronics sealed and protected from water exposure.
* **Redesigned for Commercial Production.** The prototype was adapted for injection-molded ABS manufacturing, including mold flow simulation, snap-fit assembly, and a standardized relay timer circuit to reduce cost and complexity at scale.
